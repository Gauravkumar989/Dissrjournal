About the Journal

DISSRJ — Deepak: An International Social Science Research Journal is an international, registered and refereed multi-disciplinary quarterly research journal published by JDL Publication. It provides a platform for researchers, faculty members, research scholars and professionals to publish original and quality research.

The journal encourages research that contributes to knowledge, policy and society. Papers are accepted in English and Hindi, and every paper goes through editorial screening, a plagiarism check and peer review before publication.

Publication Ethics

Authors must submit original work, cite all sources properly, disclose conflicts of interest, and not submit the same paper to more than one journal at a time.

Reviewers evaluate papers objectively and confidentially, and inform the editor of any conflict of interest or suspected misconduct.

Editors decide on publication based on merit, relevance and originality alone, and keep all submissions confidential.

Similarity should be below 10% (excluding references). Papers with higher similarity are returned for revision or rejected.

Author Guidelines

  • MS Word (.doc / .docx), A4 size, 1-inch margins.
  • English: Times New Roman 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing. Hindi: Unicode (Mangal) or Kruti Dev 010, 14 pt.
  • Length: 3,000–8,000 words including references.
  • Abstract of 150–250 words with 4–6 keywords.

Title · Author name(s), designation, institution, email and mobile · Abstract · Keywords · Introduction · Review of literature · Objectives / hypothesis · Research methodology · Analysis & discussion · Conclusion · References.

APA 7th edition is preferred. Law papers may use Bluebook or OSCOLA consistently. Every in-text citation must appear in the reference list.

The paper must be original, unpublished and not under review elsewhere. Similarity must be below 10%. On acceptance, authors sign a copyright and originality declaration.
Publication Process
  1. Submit — send your paper by email or the submission form.
  2. Screening — scope check and plagiarism report.
  3. Peer review — evaluation by subject experts.
  4. Acceptance — revisions (if any), copyright form and fee.
  5. Publication — paper published in the next issue with an e-certificate.
